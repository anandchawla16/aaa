## Clause 3 — Probation and confirmation

**3.1** All employees appointed to a permanent position serve a probation
period of **eleven (11) months** from the date of joining.

**3.2** Probation may be extended **once**, by a maximum of **three (3)
months**, by written order of the Group HR Head. A second extension is not
permitted. At the end of an extended probation the employee is either confirmed
or their services are discontinued.

**3.3** A confirmation letter must be issued within **fifteen (15) calendar
days** of the successful completion of probation.

**3.4** **Deemed confirmation.** Where no confirmation letter and no extension
order has been issued within the period in Clause 3.3, the employee shall be
**deemed confirmed** with effect from the date probation ended, and shall be
entitled to all benefits of a confirmed employee from that date.

**3.5** Employees on probation accrue Casual Leave on a pro-rata basis but do
not accrue Earned Leave.

## Clause 4 — Leave entitlement

**4.1** Leave year runs **1 April to 31 March**, coinciding with the academic
and financial year.

| Leave type | Entitlement per leave year | Accumulation | On separation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave (CL) | **8 days** | Not accumulable. Unused CL lapses on 31 March. | Not encashable |
| Sick Leave (SL) | **7 days** | Accumulable up to 21 days | Not encashable |
| Earned Leave (EL) | **15 days** (confirmed employees only) | Accumulable up to **90 days** | Encashable at basic pay |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks for the first two surviving children; 12 weeks thereafter | — | Per statute |
| Paternity Leave | 7 days, within 3 months of birth | Not accumulable | — |
| Compensatory Off | 1 day per full working Sunday / gazetted holiday worked | Must be availed within 60 days | Lapses |

**4.2** Casual Leave may not be taken for more than **three (3) consecutive
days**, and may not be combined with Earned Leave.

**4.3** Sick Leave of more than **two (2) consecutive days** requires a
registered medical practitioner's certificate.

**4.4** **Academic staff may not avail Earned Leave during the academic session
(15 June to 31 March)** except with the written approval of the Group Academic
Director. EL for academic staff is ordinarily availed during the summer break.

**4.5** Leave is not a right. All leave requires prior sanction by the reporting
authority except Sick Leave, which must be intimated on the first day of absence.

## Clause 5 — Notice period and relieving

**5.1** Notice period on resignation:

| Category | Notice period |
|---|---|
| Academic staff, confirmed | **90 days** |
| Academic staff, on probation | 30 days |
| Non-academic staff, confirmed | 60 days |
| Non-academic staff, on probation | 30 days |
| Principal / Vice Principal | 120 days |

**5.2** **Mid-session relieving bar.** No academic staff member shall be
relieved between **15 June and 31 March** irrespective of notice served, except
with the written approval of the **Board of Trustees**. Where a resignation is
tendered during this window, the notice period is deemed to run from 1 April
following.

**5.3** Notice period may be bought out at **basic pay** (not CTC) for the
unserved portion, only with Group HR Head approval. Clause 5.2 cannot be bought
out.

**5.4** No relieving letter, experience letter or Provident Fund transfer will
be processed until institutional dues, library items and issued assets are
cleared and a signed handover note is on record.

## Clause 6 — Appraisal and increment

**6.1** Annual appraisal is conducted in **February**. Increments are effective
**1 April**.

**6.2** Rating scale and consequence:

| Rating | Meaning | Increment |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Exceeds expectations | Eligible for increment up to 12% + one-time incentive |
| **B** | Meets expectations | Eligible for increment up to 8% |
| **C** | Partially meets expectations | **No increment.** Written development plan, review at 6 months |
| **D** | Below expectations | **No increment.** Formal Performance Improvement Plan for 90 days |

**6.3** An employee who has not completed **six (6) months** of service as on
31 March is not considered in that appraisal cycle.

**6.4** Two consecutive ratings of C, or a single rating of D followed by a
failed Performance Improvement Plan, will be referred to the Group HR Head for
review of continued engagement.

## Clause 9 — Data and confidentiality

**9.1** Employee personal data — including contact details, salary, bank
details, medical records and appraisal records — is confidential and may be
processed only for the purpose for which it was collected.

**9.2** Employee or student personal data **shall not be transferred to, pasted
into, or processed by any third-party system, tool or service that is not on
the Approved Systems List** maintained by the Group IT & Compliance Committee.

**9.3** Salary and appraisal data may be shared only with the employee, their
reporting authority, the Group HR Head, and the Accounts function to the extent
required for payroll.

**9.4** Any suspected disclosure of personal data must be reported to the Group
HR Head **within 24 hours** of discovery.
