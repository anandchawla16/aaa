# Meridian Group of Schools — Fee Policy & Collection Circular

**Document:** MGS/ACC/CIR/03 · **Academic Year:** 2026–27
**Issued by:** Group CFO · **Effective:** 1 April 2026
**Circulated to:** All Campus Accountants, Principals, Admin Managers

> **Synthetic document** for training use. Fictional group, fictional figures.

## 1. Fee structure and due dates

**1.1** Annual fee is billed in three terms. Due dates are:

| Term | Covers | Invoice raised | Due date |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term 1 | April – July | 26 March | **10 April** |
| Term 2 | August – October | 26 July | **10 August** |
| Term 3 | November – March | 26 October | **10 November** |

**1.2** Campuses may issue individually staggered due dates for students
admitted mid-year or on an approved instalment plan. The staggered date on the
invoice governs, not the standard date in 1.1.

**1.3** A grace period of **fifteen (15) days** applies after the due date. No
late fee accrues during grace.

## 2. Late fee

**2.1** After the grace period, a late fee of **₹50 per day** accrues on any
outstanding term balance.

**2.2** Late fee for a single term is **capped at ₹3,000**.

**2.3** **No late fee** shall be levied on:
- students admitted under the **RTE** quota;
- **Staff Ward** concession holders;
- any account where a written instalment plan approved under Clause 4.2 is
  being adhered to;
- any account under active dispute recorded in the ledger remarks.

**2.4** Late fee may be waived by the Campus Accountant up to ₹1,000 and by the
Group CFO above that. Every waiver must be recorded with a reason.

## 3. Collection escalation ladder

Escalation is measured in **days past the due date on the invoice** (not past
the grace period).

| Stage | Days past due | Action | Channel | Approved by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | +1 | Courteous reminder, no mention of penalty | Parent's preferred channel | Campus Accountant |
| R2 | +8 | Reminder stating grace period ends on day 15 | WhatsApp + Email | Campus Accountant |
| R3 | +16 | Notice that late fee has begun to accrue, with amount | Email + Call | Campus Accountant |
| R4 | +31 | Principal's letter; offer of instalment plan meeting | Email + Call | **Principal** |
| R5 | +46 | Account listed in Group CFO's weekly review | Internal | Group CFO |
| R6 | +61 | Formal demand letter | Registered post + Email | **Group CFO** |
| R7 | +90 | Case-by-case review. Any withholding of results, hall ticket or Transfer Certificate requires **written Trustee approval** and must comply with applicable board and state regulations. | — | **Board of Trustees** |

## 4. Concessions and instalments

**4.1** Published concession slabs:

| Concession | Value | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Sibling (2nd child onward) | 10% of tuition | Campus Accountant |
| Staff Ward | 50% of tuition | Group HR Head + Group CFO |
| Merit Scholarship | 25% of tuition | Group Academic Director |
| RTE | 100% as per state notification | Statutory |
| Management Concession | Case by case | **Group CFO only** |

**4.2** A Campus Accountant may approve a maximum of **two instalments** within
a term. Any plan beyond two instalments, or spanning terms, requires Group CFO
approval in writing.

**4.3** Concessions do not apply to transport, examination or activity
components unless the sanction letter says so explicitly.

## 5. Dishonoured payments

**5.1** A dishonoured cheque attracts a charge of **₹500** in addition to any
late fee.

**5.2** After one dishonoured cheque, the account moves to electronic payment
only for the remainder of the academic year.

## 6. Conduct rules for fee communication — non-negotiable

These rules exist because they have been breached before. They apply to every
reminder, letter, call script and message, whoever drafts it.

**6.1** Fee communication is addressed to the **parent or guardian on record
only**. A student is never the recipient, the messenger, or the subject of a
fee conversation.

**6.2** A student is **never** excluded from class, assembly, meals, transport
or any classroom activity for a fee reason.

**6.3** Defaulter names are **never** displayed, read out, circulated on any
group, or shared with other parents or with staff who have no collection role.

**6.4** No communication may carry a threat of any action that is not in the
ladder at Clause 3, and no stage may be skipped.

**6.5** Every outbound fee communication is **approved by a named human** before
it is sent. Drafting may be assisted; sending is never automatic.

**6.6** Language must remain courteous at every stage, including R6 and R7.
Assume the letter will be read aloud to the Board and shared on social media.
